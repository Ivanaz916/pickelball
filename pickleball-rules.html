Doubles Pickleball

Basic rules for tournament day

🎾 The Basics

  • Doubles pickleball is played 2 vs 2 on a badminton-sized court (20′ × 44′) with a net slightly lower than tennis (36″ at the sidelines, 34″ in the middle).
  • You hit a plastic ball with holes (a "pickleball") using solid paddles.
  • The ball must stay in bounds, and each side gets only one bounce max before it must be returned.

🏓 Serving

  • The serve must be hit underhand (contact below the waist), from behind the baseline.
  • Serve diagonally crosscourt — it must land in the opposite diagonal service box, beyond the kitchen.
  • The server on the right side serves first; keep serving (alternating sides) as long as your team wins points.
  • When the first server faults, the partner serves next (except at the very start of the game — the first serving team gets only one server). When both servers fault, it's a side out and the other team serves.
Calling the score: say three numbers — your score, their score, server number (1 or 2). E.g., "4 – 2 – 1".

⭐ Scoring

  • Only the serving team can score points.
  • Games are played to 11, win by 2.
  • When your team's score is even, the first server should be on the right side; odd, on the left — a handy way to catch positioning mistakes.

⛔ The Two-Bounce Rule

  1. The serve must bounce once before the receiving team returns it.
  2. The return must also bounce once before the serving team hits it.
  3. After those two bounces, anyone may volley (hit out of the air) or play off the bounce.
Tip: because of this rule, the serving team should stay back near the baseline until the third shot.

🍳 The Kitchen (Non-Volley Zone)

  • The kitchen is the 7-foot zone on each side of the net.
  • You may not volley while standing in the kitchen (or touching its line) — including if your momentum carries you in after the shot.
  • You may step into the kitchen to play a ball that has bounced — just step back out before volleying again.

❌ Faults (you lose the rally)

  • Hitting the ball out of bounds or into the net.
  • Volleying before the two-bounce rule is satisfied.
  • Volleying while touching the kitchen or its line.
  • Letting the ball bounce twice on your side.
  • Serve landing in the kitchen, on the kitchen line, or in the wrong service box.
  • Touching the net or crossing onto the opponents' side during play.

🤝 Tournament Etiquette

  • Call the score loudly before every serve.
  • Line calls are made by the receiving team — if in doubt, it's in.
  • Balls from other courts: stop play immediately and replay the point ("ball on court!").
  • Paddle-tap at the net after each game. Have fun!
